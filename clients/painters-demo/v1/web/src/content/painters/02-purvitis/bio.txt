The defining landscape painter of Latvian art. Director of the Riga City Art Museum and the first rector of the Latvian Academy of Art, Purvītis taught a generation of painters the discipline of looking at their own land. His monumental scenes of Latvian forests, rivers, and the late-winter snowmelt linked the new nation to its terrain so completely that, for a long time, to picture Latvia was to picture a Purvītis. He died in exile in Bad Nauheim in 1945, having lost the studio and much of the work to the war.
