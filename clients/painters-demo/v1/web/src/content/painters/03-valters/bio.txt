A leading figure of early Latvian impressionism. Trained in St. Petersburg and resident for much of his career in Germany, Valters painted with a loose, atmospheric touch — fishermen on the Baltic shore, market squares, sun-bleached coastlines. His later canvases broke toward a heavier, more expressionist palette, the light pulled apart into separate burning notes. He kept a Latvian eye through it all: the country shows up in the weather, even when the subject is somewhere else.
